Waterfall Escapes in Laguna

Pagsanjan Falls

Pagsanjan Falls, also known as Cavinti Falls, is one of the most famous waterfalls in Laguna. It is known for its powerful cascading waters, towering rock formations, and lush tropical surroundings, making it a top tourist destination in the province.

The falls is traditionally reached through a boat ride called “Shooting the Rapids,” where skilled boatmen navigate through the river against the current, passing through scenic cliffs and smaller rapids. The journey itself is a major highlight of the experience.

Visitors can enjoy bamboo rafting, swimming near the falls, and exploring the nearby cave behind the waterfall. It is recommended to bring waterproof bags, extra clothes, and follow safety guidelines during the boat ride and activities.

Taytay Falls

Taytay Falls, also known as Imelda Falls, is a serene waterfall located in Majayjay, Laguna. It is known for its cool, clear waters and peaceful forest surroundings, making it a relaxing destination for nature lovers.

The falls is easily accessible with a short walk down a paved path from the entrance. Visitors can enjoy swimming in its refreshing natural pool and relaxing in the calm environment surrounded by trees and rock formations.

Guests are advised to bring swimwear, extra clothes, and food for picnics. It is best to visit early in the day or on weekdays to avoid crowds and fully enjoy the quiet atmosphere.

Espasol

Espasol – A soft, cylindrical rice cake made from glutinous rice flour and coconut milk, dusted with toasted rice flour. It has a slightly chewy texture and a rich coconut flavor, making it one of Laguna’s most popular delicacies.

Where to buy: Espasol is widely available in Los Baños, especially along roadside stalls and local delicacy shops. It is commonly sold as a fresh pasalubong item.

Kesong Puti

Kesong Puti – A soft white cheese made from carabao’s milk, known for its mild salty taste and creamy texture. It is often eaten with bread or paired with native delicacies.

Where to buy: Kesong puti is commonly found in Santa Cruz, Pila, and nearby towns. It is sold in local markets and specialty dairy shops.

Uraro Cookies

Uraro (Arrowroot Cookies) – A delicate, crumbly cookie made from arrowroot flour, known for its light texture that melts in your mouth. It is a traditional delicacy often enjoyed as a snack or pasalubong.

Where to buy: Uraro is popular in Pagsanjan and nearby areas, commonly sold in local bakeries and pasalubong centers.
