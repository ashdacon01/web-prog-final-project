Laguna’s Top Waterfall Destinations

Pagsanjan Falls

Pagsanjan Falls

One of the most famous waterfalls in Laguna, known for its thrilling canoe ride through the river gorge leading to the main waterfall. Surrounded by lush forests and dramatic rock walls.

  • Location: Pagsanjan, Laguna
  • Difficulty: Easy to Moderate (canoe ride experience)
  • Height: ~91 meters
  • Trail Type: River canoe ride + short walk
  • Best Season: June – February
  • Highlights: Canoe ride, Devil’s Cave, waterfall drop view
  • Estimated Travel Time: 1–2 hours (canoe ride)
Taytay Falls

Taytay Falls (Majayjay Falls)

A cold, clear waterfall located in Majayjay, Laguna. It is known for its refreshing natural pool, peaceful forest surroundings, and relaxing atmosphere.

  • Location: Majayjay, Laguna
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Height: ~15–20 meters
  • Trail Type: Short forest walk
  • Best Season: Year-round (best during dry season)
  • Highlights: Cold spring water, natural pool, forest scenery
  • Estimated Travel Time: 30–60 minutes hike
