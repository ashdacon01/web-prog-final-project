Hidden Nature Escapes in Rizal

Masungi Georeserve

Masungi Georeserve is a protected conservation area located in Rizal, known for its dramatic limestone formations, lush forest landscapes, and unique rope courses. It is a popular eco-tourism destination that focuses on environmental protection and sustainable tourism.

The experience is guided and typically takes around 3–4 hours to complete. Visitors follow a designated trail featuring rope bridges, stone pathways, and viewing platforms such as the iconic Sapot and Tatay stations, offering panoramic views of the Sierra Madre mountain range.

Visitors are required to book in advance due to strict conservation policies. It is recommended to wear comfortable hiking shoes, bring water, and follow all guidelines to help preserve the natural environment.

Daranak Falls

Daranak Falls is one of the most popular natural attractions in Tanay, Rizal, known for its clear cascading waters and lush forest surroundings. It is a well-loved destination for swimming, picnics, and nature trips.

The falls is easily accessible with a short walk from the entrance, making it suitable for all types of visitors. The main waterfall forms a wide natural pool where guests can swim and relax while enjoying the refreshing mountain atmosphere.

Visitors are advised to bring swimwear, extra clothes, and waterproof bags for belongings. Weekdays are recommended for fewer crowds, and safety rules must be followed when swimming in deeper areas.

Suman sa Ibos

Suman – A traditional Filipino rice cake made from glutinous rice and coconut milk, wrapped in palm or banana leaves. It has a soft, slightly sticky texture and is often paired with sugar or ripe mango.

Where to buy: Suman is widely sold in Rizal, especially in Antipolo and Tanay. It is commonly found in roadside stalls, public markets, and pasalubong centers.

Cashew Nuts

Kasoy (Cashew Nuts) – Antipolo is known for its locally produced cashew nuts, often roasted, salted, or caramelized. These are popular snacks and are commonly bought as pasalubong by visitors.

Where to buy: Kasoy is widely available in Antipolo City, especially near churches, tourist areas, and roadside pasalubong shops.

Sapin-Sapin

Sapin-sapin – A colorful layered rice cake made from glutinous rice flour, coconut milk, and sugar. It is soft, chewy, and often topped with latik (coconut caramel bits).

Where to buy: These delicacies are commonly sold in Antipolo and nearby towns, especially in local markets and pasalubong centers along major roads.
