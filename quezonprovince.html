Off-the-Grid Islands in Quezon Province

Polillo Island

Polillo Island is a group of islands located in Quezon Province, known for its quiet coastal communities, rich marine biodiversity, and untouched natural beauty. It is a peaceful destination ideal for nature lovers seeking a less commercialized island experience.

The island is accessible by boat from mainland Quezon (commonly via Real port). Visitors can enjoy white-sand beaches, fishing villages, and activities such as swimming, snorkeling, and island exploration. Travel time varies depending on sea conditions.

Visitors are advised to bring essentials such as food, water, and cash, as facilities are limited in some areas. It is best to plan trips in advance and respect local communities and marine environments to help preserve the island’s natural beauty.

Jomalig Island

Jomalig Island is one of the most remote and scenic islands in Quezon Province, famous for its golden-yellow beaches, crystal-clear waters, and peaceful atmosphere. It is often described as a hidden paradise far from crowded tourist spots.

Reaching Jomalig usually involves a boat ride from Real, Quezon, followed by several hours of travel depending on sea conditions. Once on the island, visitors can explore beaches like Kanaway and Salibungot, go island hopping, or simply enjoy the untouched coastal scenery.

Travelers are advised to prepare essentials such as food, water, cash, and sun protection, as facilities are limited. It is best suited for planned overnight trips or guided tours due to its remote location.

QUEZON PROVINCE DELICACY

Budin Cassava Cake
Longganisang Lucban
Hardinera

Budin (Cassava Cake) – A soft and chewy cassava cake made from grated cassava, coconut milk, and sugar. It is often topped with cheese or coconut strips and is a popular pasalubong delicacy in Quezon Province, especially in Lucban.

Where to buy: Budin is widely sold in local bakeries, roadside stalls, and pasalubong centers in Lucban and nearby towns. It is commonly bought by tourists visiting the province.

Longganisang Lucban – A famous garlicky and slightly sour sausage known for its small size and bold flavor. It is traditionally made with native spices and has a reddish color.

Where to buy: This delicacy is widely available in Lucban, especially in markets, specialty shops, and during festivals like Pahiyas.

Hardinera – A festive meatloaf dish made with ground pork, eggs, vegetables, and pineapple. It is steamed in a llanera and often served during special occasions and fiestas.

Where to buy: Hardinera is commonly found in Lucban and nearby towns, especially during celebrations and in local carinderias and food shops.
