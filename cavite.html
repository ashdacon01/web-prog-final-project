Top 2 Hiking Mountains in Cavite

Pico de Loro

Pico de Loro

Famous for its monolith cliff and panoramic coastal views. One of the most iconic hiking destinations in Cavite–Batangas, known for its mix of forest trails and summit rock climbing.

  • Location: Maragondon, Cavite / Nasugbu, Batangas
  • Difficulty: Moderate to Hard
  • Height: ~664 MASL
  • Trail Type: Forest + rocky ascent
  • Best Season: December – May (dry months)
  • Highlights: Monolith peak, coastal view, dense forest trail
  • Estimated Hiking Time: 4–6 hours (round trip)
Mt. Marami

Mt. Marami

A scenic hiking destination in Cavite known for its dramatic rock formations called “Silyang Bato” and wide summit views overlooking Manila Bay, Bataan, and nearby provinces.

  • Location: Maragondon, Cavite
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Height: ~405 MASL
  • Trail Type: Grassland + river crossing + rocky summit
  • Best Season: November – May (dry months)
  • Highlights: Silyang Bato formation, sunset views, sea of clouds (rare)
  • Estimated Hiking Time: 3–5 hours (round trip)
