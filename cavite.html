Mountain Escapes in Cavite

Pico de Loro

One of Cavite’s most iconic hiking destinations, known for its scenic trails, limestone formations, and the famous Monolith at the summit with panoramic views of the sea and surrounding mountains.

The hike is considered moderate and usually takes 4–6 hours round trip. Climbers should expect steep and rocky sections, especially near the summit. Registration is required at the base, and guides may be needed depending on current regulations.

Hikers are advised to bring enough water, sun protection, proper hiking shoes, and light snacks. Early morning climbs are recommended to avoid heat and crowds, and overnight camping may be subject to permits.

Mt. Marami

Mt. Marami is one of Cavite’s most popular hiking destinations, known for its sharp limestone formations, scenic grasslands, and the iconic Silyang Bato at the summit. It offers a challenging but rewarding trail with stunning views of nearby mountains and the sea.

The hike is considered moderate to difficult and usually takes 5–7 hours round trip depending on pace. Climbers should expect long river crossings, rocky ascents, and exposed trails under the sun.

Hikers are advised to bring enough water, sun protection, trekking shoes, and light snacks. Early morning start is highly recommended, and camping is allowed at designated areas for overnight hikes.

CALABARZON DELICACY

Bacalao
Puto Bumbong
Tamales

Bacalao – A traditional salt cod dish simmered with potatoes, bell peppers, tomatoes, and olive oil. Introduced during Spanish influence, it is commonly served during special occasions and family gatherings in Cavite.

Puto Bumbong – A traditional Filipino rice cake made from purple glutinous rice, steamed in bamboo tubes. It is topped with butter, grated coconut, and brown sugar, and is especially popular during the Christmas season.

Tamales – A savory delicacy made with rice flour, chicken, peanuts, and eggs, wrapped in banana leaves. It has a soft texture and a rich, slightly nutty flavor.
