CALABARZON REGION

RICH CULTURE & DESTINATIONS

CALABARZON (Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal, and Quezon) is a vibrant region in Southern Luzon known for its historical landmarks, natural attractions, thriving industries, and diverse culture.

Cavite Favorites

Bacalao – A traditional salt cod dish simmered with potatoes, bell peppers, tomatoes, and olive oil. Introduced during Spanish influence, it is commonly served during special occasions and family gatherings in Cavite.

Laguna Favorites

Buko Pie – A famous Laguna delicacy made with young coconut meat, sweet cream filling, and flaky golden crust. Originating in Los Baños, it is best known for its soft texture and fresh coconut flavor.

Batangas Favorites

Bulalo – A rich beef marrow soup slow-cooked for hours until tender. Made with corn, cabbage, and bone marrow, it is especially popular in Tagaytay due to its cool climate.

Rizal Favorites

Suman – A traditional rice cake made from sticky rice cooked in coconut milk and wrapped in banana or palm leaves. Often paired with sugar or ripe mango, it is a common pasalubong from Rizal towns.

Quezon Favorites

Pancit Habhab – A signature Lucban noodle dish served on banana leaves and eaten without utensils. Made with sautéed noodles, pork, and vegetables, it is traditionally topped with vinegar for extra flavor.
